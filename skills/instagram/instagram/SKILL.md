## Live account law (Silph Scope, 2026-07)

**Observed:** Ascended Heroes / Mega Gengar myth-bust ≈ 1.6k views, ~10 DM shares.  
Clean comparison / concentration / number-upside Reels: **~70–80% skip**, die in test pool.

Until skip rate on new posts is consistently under ~40–45%:

1. **Gate = first 1–3s** (skip). Nothing mid-reel matters if bounce is 60%+.
2. Prefer **belief confrontation** over clean insight density on frame 0.
3. Prefer **visual rejection** (WRONG / DIDN'T CRASH / red penalty chip) over calm +green badges.
4. **Portable rule** after conflict → saves/sends.
5. Innovate *inside* confrontation energy — do not clone Gengar frame-for-frame; do not revert to soft data layouts as the open.
